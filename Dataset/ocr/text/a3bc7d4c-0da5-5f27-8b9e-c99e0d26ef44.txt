In 1961, the US military's Agent Orange spraying campaign in South Viet Nam was launched

under the codename "Operation Ranch Hand."

The herbicides, particularly Agent Orange with its high dioxin content, used since 1961 have

contaminated nearly 25% of Viet Nam s ecological environment. Dioxin accumulates in aquatic

animals, especially fish, and poses a threat to humans. Dioxin causes food crops and fruits to

become barren and unproductive, depriving farmers of their economic livelihood. Former air

force site

s, where agent orange and herbicides were stored and mixed, have become dioxin
