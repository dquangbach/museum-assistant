Wet rice cultivation is the main, traditional economy of the people in Quang region. The schedule of

annual rice crops, based on the lunar calendar, lasts from the tenth lunar month of one year until the

eighth lunar month of the next. Two crops are grown: winter-spring and summer-autumn, in a sequence

of tilling the land, sowing seeds, transplanting seedlings and caring for the rice paddies until harvest.

Production methods were rudimentary, manual, and highly dependent on nature. A notable feature of

traditional agriculture in the Quang region was the combination of water management with “irrigation”

using a network of canals.

During the off-season, farmers took advantage of the time to catch freshwater fish in flooded fields,

ponds, and riverbanks. Fishing methods were diverse, using a variety of tools such as heckles, nets,

fishing-tackles, fishing rods and baskets.
