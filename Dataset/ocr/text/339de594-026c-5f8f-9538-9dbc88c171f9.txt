The Nho Ban weapons factory was established on April 15, 1946, under the direction of the Da Nang City

Party Committee to manufacture and repair weapons, ensuring a timely supply to meet combat

demands. The factory was located in Quang Nam province. Initially, it was situated in Thanh Chiem

village (Dien Ban), before being relocated to My Luoc village (Duy Xuyen), and then to Dong An village

and Phuoc Son village in (Que Son), where it bore the new name of XO10. The factory had two main

products, i.e. the modification of American pineapple grenades and old French grenades to suit our

fighting style.
