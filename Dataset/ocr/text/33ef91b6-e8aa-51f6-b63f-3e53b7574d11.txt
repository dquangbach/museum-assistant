Da Nang Airport was once used in both the “Ranch Hand" and "Pacer Ivy" campaigns, where large amounts

of dioxin leaked. Spraying equipment used during missions dispersed remaining chemicals, which were

then washed at the end of the runway, making it a heavily contaminated site with dioxin.

From 1997 to 2007, Viet Nam's Chemical Corps built several structures to isolate, contain, and temporarily

restrict the spread of dioxin into the surrounding environment. By 2012, the United States Agency for

Environmental Remediation Project at Da Nang Airport, funded at $110 million. The project implemented

the most advanced technologies. After 6 years, the project successfully treated over 90.000 cubic meters

of contaminated soil and sediment with high dioxin concentrations using thermal absorption and safely
